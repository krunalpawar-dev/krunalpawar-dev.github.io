BEFORE WE GET STARTED

questions.

Have another question?
Do you work with clients outside India?+

Yes. I work remotely with businesses in India and internationally. We agree on communication, review milestones and a practical meeting schedule before starting.

Can you improve an existing Laravel application?+

Yes. I can investigate bugs, add features, review performance and help with maintenance. I start by understanding the codebase and the affected business workflow.

How do you estimate a project?+

We discuss your requirements, priorities, integrations and timeline. I then define the scope and assumptions before preparing a quotation. The initial project discussion is free.

Can you handle the complete application?+

My work spans backend development, interfaces, database design, APIs and deployment. We agree on the exact responsibilities and deliverables for your project before development begins.

What happens after launch?+

Deployment, handover and support expectations are agreed as part of the project. Ongoing maintenance and new features can be scoped separately as your requirements evolve.
