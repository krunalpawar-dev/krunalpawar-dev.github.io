Project enquiry privacy.

A straightforward explanation of what happens when you get in touch.

What you share

The enquiry form collects your name, contact information, optional company details and project requirements. Please share only what is needed for an initial project discussion.

Why it is collected

Krunal Pawar uses these details to review your enquiry, respond through your preferred contact method and discuss a potential software development project. Submitting an enquiry does not subscribe you to marketing messages.

Storage and notifications

This GitHub Pages website sends enquiries to Formspree, the same form service used by the previous portfolio. Formspree processes your submission for delivery to the developer. GitHub Pages does not run the private application database or content manager. You can also contact the developer directly by email or WhatsApp.

Essential session data

This static portfolio does not use application sign-in or session cookies. It does not include advertising trackers. When you submit the contact form, Formspree may apply its own anti-spam and service protections.

Questions and deletion requests

To ask about your enquiry information or request its deletion, contact kmpawar0004@gmail.com. Information needed for an active project may be handled under the separate arrangements agreed for that work.
