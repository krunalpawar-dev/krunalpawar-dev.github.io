SOFTWARE THAT FITS THE WAY YOU WORK

Your business has enough moving parts.
Let’s make the software simpler.

I’m Krunal, a software developer working professionally since 2022. I help business owners turn repeated tasks, scattered records and growing workloads into clear, connected web applications.

You can come to me with a spreadsheet, an existing website or an idea. I’ll help you define what the system needs to do, then build the interface, database and business logic that bring it together. You work directly with the person developing your software.

My completed work includes Bharat Medical Hall’s back-office system for staff and daily operations, Treeva Healthcare’s website and clinic platform for appointments and administration, and KumbhSnaan’s personalized ritual booking platform.

Whether you need a new application or improvements to an existing one, we’ll start with your priorities and agree on a practical scope. My focus is software your team can understand, use and build on as your business grows.
