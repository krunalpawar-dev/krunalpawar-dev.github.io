Warehouse management

Organize receiving, storage, picking and stock counts, with barcode support and connections to your other systems.

Request a project discussion

The business challenge

Warehouse teams need a consistent view of what arrived, where it was stored and what is ready to leave.

How I approach it

I build workflows around warehouse locations and inventory movements. Barcode-led steps, cycle counts and integration checkpoints help teams identify exceptions before they spread across connected systems.

What we can build

  • Inbound and putaway
  • Storage and retrieval
  • Inventory tracking
  • Cycle counting
  • Barcode integration
  • ERP and TMS integration

How your project will work

We discuss what your team does today and which tasks the software should help with. Before work starts, we agree on the features, estimated cost, timeline and how you will review the work. We also discuss getting your team started and any support needed after launch.

Working with your team
