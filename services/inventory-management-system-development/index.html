Stock & order management

Track products, purchases, sales and stock transfers so your team can see where items are.

Request a project discussion

The business challenge

Manual stock updates can leave teams with conflicting counts and little visibility into how inventory changed.

How I approach it

We define the stock movement model and validation rules first. Receiving, dispatch and transfers update inventory through recorded transactions so teams can trace adjustments and investigate differences.

What we can build

  • Products and stock
  • Warehouses
  • Purchase and sales orders
  • Barcode management
  • Stock transfers
  • Stock movement history

How your project will work

We discuss what your team does today and which tasks the software should help with. Before work starts, we agree on the features, estimated cost, timeline and how you will review the work. We also discuss getting your team started and any support needed after launch.

Working with your team

I collaborate remotely with businesses in India and internationally. We agree on communication, review sessions and responsibilities early, so you can make informed decisions throughout the project.
