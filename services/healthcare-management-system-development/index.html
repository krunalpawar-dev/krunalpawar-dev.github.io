The business challenge

A public website may collect enquiries while the operations team handles appointments, billing and staff in disconnected tools.

How I approach it

I connect enquiry capture with a purpose-built administration platform. Patient information and operational functions receive scoped permissions, and workflows are designed around the staff who use them.

What we can build

  • Patients and appointments
  • Enquiries
  • Billing and invoices
  • Expenses
  • Staff and services
  • Role-based permissions

How your project will work

We discuss what your team does today and which tasks the software should help with. Before work starts, we agree on the features, estimated cost, timeline and how you will review the work. We also discuss getting your team started and any support needed after launch.
