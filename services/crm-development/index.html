The business challenge

When lead information lives in separate spreadsheets and conversations, follow-ups are easy to miss and sales activity is difficult to understand.

How I approach it

I bring customer records, ownership and next actions into one workflow. The pipeline reflects your sales process, so the team can see what needs attention without assembling information from multiple tools.

What we can build

  • Lead management
  • Customer records
  • Sales pipeline
  • Follow-ups and tasks
  • Activity history
  • Notifications

From requirements to release

We begin with your workflow, users, integrations and priorities. Together we define the first release, review milestones and acceptance criteria. Development, testing and deployment follow that agreed scope, with a clear discussion about ongoing support.

Working with your team

I collaborate remotely with businesses in India and internationally. We agree on communication, review sessions and responsibilities early, so you can make informed decisions throughout the project.
