Project overview

Track goods from arrival to storage and dispatch, with stock counts and barcode support. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

Warehouse operations depend on accurate location and movement information. Receiving, storing and retrieving stock must remain consistent with the inventory data used by connected ERP and transport systems.

The solution

The WMS organizes inventory through inbound, putaway, storage and retrieval workflows. Cycle counting and barcode integration support physical operations, with ERP and TMS integration connecting warehouse events to surrounding business systems.
