Concept overview

A concept SaaS workspace for property enquiries, bookings, tenants, payments, maintenance and owner reporting. This self-directed concept demonstrates how I would approach the product, workflows and technical structure; it is not presented as commissioned client work.

The business challenge

Property managers can lose visibility when leads, booking calendars, tenant records, maintenance requests and payment follow-ups are handled in different tools.

The proposed solution

The StayPilot concept brings the customer journey and property operations together. A shared calendar would connect availability and bookings, while tenant, payment and maintenance records stay attached to the relevant unit and organization.

Capabilities demonstrated

  • Property and unit management
  • Lead and enquiry pipeline
  • Booking and occupancy calendar
  • Tenant profiles and agreements
  • Rent, deposits and payment tracking
  • Maintenance requests and assignments
  • Owner dashboards and statements
  • Multi-organization administration
