Concept overview

A concept HR platform connecting employee records, attendance exceptions, leave approvals and reviewable payroll runs. This self-directed concept demonstrates how I would approach the product, workflows and technical structure; it is not presented as commissioned client work.

The business challenge

Attendance devices, leave requests, salary spreadsheets and employee documents often live in separate places. HR teams then spend time reconciling exceptions before every payroll run.

The proposed solution

The PeopleFlow concept creates one reviewable path from employee and shift data through attendance, leave approvals and payroll. Exception queues would help HR resolve missing punches or policy conflicts before salary calculations are finalized.

Capabilities demonstrated

  • Employee records and onboarding
  • Biometric attendance integration
  • Shift and roster management
  • Leave, holidays and comp-off workflows
  • Timesheets and manager approvals
  • Salary structures and payroll runs
  • Payslips and employee self-service
  • Role-based reports and audit history
