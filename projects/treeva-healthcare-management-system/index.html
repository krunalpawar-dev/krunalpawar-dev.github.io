Project overview

A healthcare website for patient enquiries, connected to appointment, billing and staff management. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

The public-facing experience needed to explain healthcare services and collect patient enquiries and appointment requests. The internal team also needed a place to manage those requests alongside patient records, billing and daily operations.

The solution

The project combines a lead-focused public website with a healthcare administration platform. Enquiries and appointment requests become inputs to the operational workflow, while billing, services and staff are managed within the admin system.

Features developed

  • Patient management
  • Appointment management
  • Enquiry management
  • Billing and invoices
  • Expense management
  • Service management
  • Staff management
  • Users and permissions
