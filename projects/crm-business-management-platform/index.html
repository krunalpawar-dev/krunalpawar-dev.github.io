Project overview

Customer details, sales leads, tasks and follow-ups together for the sales team. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

Sales teams need visibility into lead ownership, previous conversations and the next action. When those details are separated, following up consistently becomes difficult.

The solution

CRM functionality connects leads and customers with tasks, follow-ups and activity history. Sales process management gives the team a shared view of where an opportunity stands and what should happen next.
