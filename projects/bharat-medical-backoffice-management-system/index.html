Project overview

Orders, employee details and attendance in one place for a medical retail business. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

Medical retail operations involve more than fulfilling orders. Staff records, attendance and administrative responsibilities also need a consistent home. The project addressed the need to manage these internal processes from a centralized platform.

The solution

The back-office application brings order, employee and attendance management together, with role-based access to administrative functions. The structure gives the team one place to manage operational records.
