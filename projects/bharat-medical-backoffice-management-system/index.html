Project overview

A complete website and back-office platform for Bharat Medical Hall in Baripada, Odisha, bringing employee, financial and daily business operations into one place. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

Bharat Medical Hall needed a centralized way to manage daily operations, employees, finances and administrative work. The system was designed to reduce manual work and make business activities easier to track.

The solution

I built a connected business management platform with ten modules. Staff can manage orders, customers, suppliers and challans alongside attendance, leave, payroll, payslips, projects, tasks and expenses. An operations dashboard brings staff availability and sales orders into a consolidated view.

Features developed

  • Order Management
  • Employee Attendance Management
  • Leave Management
  • Payroll & Salary Management
  • Salary Payslip Generation
  • Project & Task Management
  • Challan Management
  • Supplier Management
  • Customer Management
  • Expense Management
