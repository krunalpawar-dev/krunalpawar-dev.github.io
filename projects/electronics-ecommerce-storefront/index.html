Project overview

A catalogue-led electronics storefront with promotional collections, category browsing, product filters and customer enquiries. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

A broad electronics catalogue needs a clear way for shoppers to move from featured promotions into focused product browsing without losing access to categories, brands and availability filters.

The solution

I built a retail storefront that combines campaign-led landing content with a filterable shop grid. Product status, category and brand options help visitors narrow the catalogue, while contact and account areas support the wider customer journey.

Features developed

  • Promotional product collections
  • Category navigation
  • Product catalogue grid
  • Stock and sale filters
  • Brand-based browsing
  • Product ratings and pricing
  • Customer contact form
  • Responsive storefront layout
