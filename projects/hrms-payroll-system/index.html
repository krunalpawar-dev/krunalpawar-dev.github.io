Project overview

Attendance, leave policies and payroll brought together in a structured employee management system. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

Payroll depends on more than a total of hours worked. Shift assignments, biometric events, leave balances, comp off and policy exceptions all affect the inputs that must be reviewed.

The solution

The HRMS connects employee records, attendance, leave and timesheets with payroll preparation. Salary templates and payslip generation support a repeatable payroll workflow, while policy handling accounts for the rules that shape attendance and leave decisions.
