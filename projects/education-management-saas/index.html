Project overview

Student records, attendance, fees and academic administration for educational institutions. This case study outlines the application’s purpose, the functionality developed and the operational context behind the work.

The client problem

Educational institutions need to coordinate student information, staff, attendance, academics and fees. A SaaS model adds the requirement to serve multiple institutions while maintaining appropriate organizational boundaries.

The solution

The platform groups institutional workflows within a SaaS application. Tenant-aware access and shared application architecture provide a foundation for adding institutions without duplicating the product for each organization.
